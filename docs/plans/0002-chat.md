# 0002 — Chat: site and team groups

Status: draft
Brief: none

## Intent

WhatsApp groups are created and maintained by hand, so their membership drifts from reality - they might keep members which are not a real employees long ago or have no new incoming members at all for a long time. Here every site has a group and every team at a site has a group, and membership follows the member record: a new hire is in the right groups on day one, someone moved to another site is moved with no one doing it (by the system not a manager), and a leaver is gone the moment they are deactivated.

Nobody manages group membership.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A member sees exactly two groups: their site's group and their site-and-team group. They see no other groups.
2. A member added to a site and team sees those groups and their full message history without any further action.
3. A member whose site or team changes stops seeing the old groups and sees the new ones, without any further action.
4. A message posted to a group appears for every other member viewing that group, without reloading.
5. A member cannot read or post to a group they do not belong to.
6. A message is text, 1 to 2000 characters. An empty or longer message is rejected with a visible and human-readable validation error.
7. Each group shows an unread count for the member; opening the group clears it.

## Out of scope

- Direct messages, ad-hoc groups, group admin.
- Media, files, reactions, replies, editing, deleting.
- Push notifications. The next thing to build.
- Search, typing indicators, presence.
